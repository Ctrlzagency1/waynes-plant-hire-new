Our story

Wayne's Plant Hire World was founded to provide well-maintained construction and earthmoving equipment to contractors and developers across the West Rand.

We know downtime on a construction project is lost money. Based in Randfontein, we're positioned to deliver equipment quickly to your site. Our fleet has grown to include dozers, excavators, graders and specialised trucks.
